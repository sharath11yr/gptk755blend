# Idea for later: earning from clipping (saved 13 Nov 2026)

Clipping = cutting other creators'/brands' long videos into Shorts/Reels and getting paid per views.
Rates below are what a social post claimed - NOT verified. Check reviews before joining.

| Platform | Claimed pay | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| vyro.com | $1–2.50 per 1K views | weekly payouts, $10 minimum |
| contentrewards.com | $1–1.25 per 1K verified views | some per-post/retainer campaigns, withdraw from $1 |
| clipping.net | $0.40–3 per 1K views | brand-funded, many platforms |
| clipster.com | ~$1 per 1K (some up to $3) | campaign marketplace |
| clipur.com | ~$1.50 per 1K | creator campaigns: X, TikTok, Instagram, YT Shorts |
| promote.fun | $0.50–3 per 1K | crypto/memecoin - AVOID |
| trapster.gg | $1–5 per 1K | gaming studios |
| darebay.com | $0.10–2 per 1K | gaming/creator tasks, $10 USDT minimum |
| nexxi.io | varies | brand clipper communities - check access first |

## Our notes
- $1,000/month at ~$1 per 1K views = about 1 million views every month.
- Never pay to join. Avoid crypto/memecoin campaigns.
- Paid clips must be labelled as promotion (#ad / paid partnership).
- Use ONE separate account, never the 4 GetYr channels. No bots / mass accounts (platform spam rules).
- Test one platform by hand for 2 weeks first. If it really pays, GetYr Studio could later auto-cut
  campaign videos into captioned Shorts.
